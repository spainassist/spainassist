FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to the most common questions before starting your process.

How long does the NIE process take?

It depends on appointment availability and your province. In many cases it takes from a few days to several weeks. We keep you updated at every stage so you always know where things stand.

Do I need to be in Spain for the process?

Some steps may require your physical presence. We explain exactly what applies to your case before you begin, so there are no surprises when you arrive.

Can you help me open a bank account in Spain?

Yes. We help you choose the right bank, review the required documents and guide you through the full process from start to finish.

Do you work with all nationalities?

Yes. We have experience working with clients from over 20 countries. Requirements vary by nationality and we tailor our guidance accordingly.

How do I pay for the service?

Payment details are confirmed once we have reviewed your case. We accept bank transfer and other common methods. There is no payment required before we assess your situation.

How quickly will I get a response?

We typically respond within 2 hours on business days. For urgent cases, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.
